BPF, eBPF and the eunomia-bpf projects

A chapter-wise guide: what the Berkeley Packet Filter and its extended form are, how they work inside the Linux kernel, and what every actively maintained eunomia-bpf project does, with diagrams, real code, papers and quizzes.

8 chaptersStart with Chapter 01 if eBPF is new to you

Part I · Foundations

01 · What is BPF? From packet filter to in-kernel VM

How a 1993 packet filter became Linux's safe in-kernel execution engine: classic BPF, seccomp-bpf, the 2014 eBPF rewrite, and a first hello world.

02 · The eBPF Instruction Set and Virtual Machine

The eBPF instruction set: eleven 64-bit registers, the calling convention, the 8-byte instruction format and its classes, atomics, BPF-to-BPF calls, the 512-byte stack, the interpreter and x86-64 JIT, constant blinding, and the RFC 9669 standard.

03 · The eBPF verifier

How the in-kernel verifier proves an eBPF program safe: the CFG pass, path-by-path abstract interpretation, register types, tnum and range tracking, pruning, precision, loops, limits, Spectre hardening, reading error messages, and the research that checks the verifier itself.

04 · Maps, helpers and kfuncs

How eBPF programs keep state in maps, stream events to user space through the ring buffer, and call into the kernel through stable helpers, unstable kfuncs, BPF-to-BPF calls and tail calls.

05 · Program types and hooks: where eBPF runs

Where eBPF programs attach — kprobes, trampolines, tracepoints, uprobes/USDT, perf sampling, LSM, cgroups, struct_ops, sched_ext, iterators and HID-BPF — and what each hook can see, change and cost.

06 · BTF, CO-RE and libbpf

How BTF type information, compiler-emitted CO-RE relocations and the libbpf loader let one compiled eBPF object run on many kernels, and how skeletons, links and pinning manage its lifecycle.

07 · eBPF in the networking stack: XDP, AF_XDP, tc/TCX and socket programs

Where eBPF hooks into the Linux packet path, from XDP in the NIC driver through AF_XDP, tc/TCX and socket-level programs, with real drop, redirect and tc programs and the commands to load them.

08 · The eBPF ecosystem map, and where eunomia-bpf fits

One exec tracer written in bpftrace, BCC, libbpf, cilium/ebpf, aya, libbpf-rs and eunomia-bpf; the major eBPF projects and runtimes; and a map of the eunomia-bpf organization that the rest of the curriculum follows.
